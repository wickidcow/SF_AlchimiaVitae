## 🌿 What does AlchimiaVitae do?

AlchimiaVitae expands Slimefun with a progression path built around **alchemy, herbalism, magical plants, experience, custom metals, potions, and infusion crafting**.

Highlights include:

- **Soul Collector** — improves mob experience rewards and can produce Condensed Souls.
- **Light & Dark Magic Plants** — magical plant resources created through infusion.
- **Plant Infusion Chamber** — creates and processes special plant materials.
- **EXP Crystallizer & EXP Crystals** — converts experience into useful alchemical resources.
- **Alchemical metals** — including Illumium, Darksteel, Mystery Metal, and related materials.
- **Divine Altar** — advanced transmutation and crafting.
- **Cosmic Cauldron** — produces specialized brews and magical concoctions.
- **Altar of Infusion** — infuses equipment with special abilities and effects.

The goal of this fork is preservation first: keep the addon recognizable while maintaining it for modern Slimefun Legacy servers.
